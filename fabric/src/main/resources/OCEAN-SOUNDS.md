These three original ambience loops were authored using deterministic noise filtering
and periodic swell envelopes in scripts/make_ocean_audio.py. They are synthesized
surf textures, not field recordings or neural audio/music generation. No third-party
recordings are included. The generated OGG assets are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0-1.0. Rebuilding audio is optional; normal Gradle builds use the bundled files.

Aurora drift is a separate original 32-second synthesized pad/wind/glass-tone loop,
authored by scripts/make_aurora_audio.py and likewise dedicated under CC0-1.0.
It is imagined aurora-themed ambience, not a recording of northern lights.

## Worlds (1.9)
Deep space, City rain and Forest breeze are original 32-second periodic DSP compositions generated by `scripts/make_world_audio.py`: low orbital pads, steady rainfall/distant traffic, and wind with bird-like tones. No recordings, speech, third-party samples or neural audio model. Released CC0 alongside the existing ambience.

## Seasons (1.11)
Desert wind, Snow hush, Abyssal drift, Garden chimes and Distant storm are original 32-second periodic DSP compositions from `scripts/make_season_audio.py`. Filtered wind, soft ice/bell tones, low underwater pads and distant rain/rumble. No recordings or third-party samples; CC0.
